## Getting Started

1. Download the Good Taste app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).
2. Open the app and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. You'll be prompted to sign in or create an account.

## Creating an Account

You can create an account using:

### Email Registration

1. On the login screen, tap "Sign Up".
2. Enter your email address and create a secure password.
3. Tap "Create Account".
4. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
5. Complete your profile with your name and phone number.

### Google Sign-In

1. On the login screen, tap "Continue with Google".
2. Select your Google account from the list or enter your credentials.
3. Review the permissions and tap "Allow".
4. Complete your profile by adding any missing information.

## Finding Restaurants

### Browse Restaurants

1. On the home screen, you'll see a list of featured restaurants.
2. Scroll through the list to explore available options.
3. Use the search bar at the top to find specific restaurants by name.
4. Tap on the filter icon to refine your search by:
   - Cuisine type
   - Price range
   - Rating
   - Location

### View Restaurant Details

1. Tap on a restaurant card to view its details page.
2. Here you can see:
   - Restaurant photos
   - Description
   - Address and contact information
   - Menu items with prices
   - Reviews and ratings
   - Available reservation times

## Making a Reservation

1. From the restaurant details page, tap "Reserve a Table".
2. Select your desired date using the calendar.
3. Choose an available time slot.
4. Specify the number of guests (1-20).
5. Add any special requests or notes (optional).
6. Review your reservation details.
7. Tap "Confirm & Continue".
8. You'll be taken to the payment screen.
9. Complete the payment process via Momo.
10. Once payment is successful, you'll receive a confirmation screen with your reservation details.

## Managing Your Reservations

### View Your Reservations

1. Tap on the "Reservations" tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. You'll see a list of all your reservations sorted by date.
3. Each reservation card shows:
   - Restaurant name
   - Date and time
   - Number of guests
   - Reservation status (Pending, Confirmed, Finished, Cancelled)

### Cancel a Reservation

1. On the "Reservations" screen, find the reservation you wish to cancel.
2. Tap on the reservation card to view details.
3. Tap the "Cancel Reservation" button.
4. Confirm your decision when prompted.
5. The reservation status will update to "Cancelled".

**Note**: Cancellation policies vary by restaurant. Some may charge a cancellation fee if done within 24 hours of the reservation time.

## Leaving Reviews

1. After your reservation date has passed, the status will change to "Finished".
2. On the "Reservations" screen, find your finished reservation.
3. Tap on the reservation card.
4. Tap "Leave a Review".
5. Rate your experience (1-5 stars).
6. Write your review in the comment box.
7. Tap "Submit Review".
8. Your review will appear on the restaurant's page.

## Managing Your Profile

1. Tap on the "Profile" tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Here you can:
   - Update your personal information
   - Change your profile picture
   - Update your password
   - Manage your payment methods
   - View your favorite restaurants
   - Access app settings

### Update Personal Information

1. On the Profile screen, tap "Edit Profile".
2. Update your name, email, or phone number.
3. Tap "Save" to confirm changes.

### Change Profile Picture

1. On the Profile screen, tap your profile picture.
2. Choose "Take Photo" or "Choose from Gallery".
3. Adjust the image as needed.
4. Tap "Save" to update your profile picture.

## Payment Process

### Adding Payment for a Reservation

1. After confirming your reservation details, you'll proceed to the payment screen.
2. The total amount will be displayed.
3. Tap "Pay with Momo".
4. You'll be redirected to the Momo payment interface.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your payment.
6. After successful payment, you'll be returned to the app with a confirmation message.

### Payment Failure

If your payment fails:
1. You'll see an error message with details.
2. You can choose to:
   - Try again with the same payment method
   - Choose a different payment method
   - Cancel the reservation process

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

1. Ensure you're using the correct email and password.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. If you forgot your password, tap "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions.
4. If problems persist, contact support via the app or email at support@goodtaste.com.

### Reservation Issues

If you encounter problems with your reservation:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Restart the app and try again.
3. Contact the restaurant directly using the phone number provided in the restaurant details.
4. Contact customer support via the "Help" section in your profile.

### Payment Problems

If you experience payment issues:
1. Ensure your Momo account is active and has sufficient funds.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Try again after a few minutes.
4. If the problem persists, use the "Contact Support" option in the app.
